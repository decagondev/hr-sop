# Quick reference — Hire for an open role: requisition to signed contract

_Take an approved hiring need from an open requisition through sourcing, structured selection, a conditional offer, compliant screening, and a fully executed employment agreement — consistently, defensibly, and with the candidate experience and legal obligations handled at every step._

| # | Step | Owner | Verify |
| - | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open and document the requisition | Hiring Manager | A requisition record exists in the ATS with role, level, reporting line, and business case. |
| 2 | Approve headcount and budget | Compensation / Finance Approver | The requisition shows an approval record with approver, date, and budgeted band. |
| 3 | Finalize the job description and selection criteria | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | Every listed requirement is job-related and consistent with business necessity; the scorecard defines competencies with behavioral anchors. |
| 4 | Source candidates | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | The role is posted to all required channels and a candidate pipeline is building in the ATS. |
| 5 | Screen applications | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | All applicants are screened against the same criteria and screening decisions are logged. |
| 6 | Recruiter phone screen | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | Each candidate is screened with the same core questions; notes are recorded in the ATS. |
| 7 | Administer job-related assessments | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | The same assessment is sent to all candidates for the role and results are recorded. |
| 8 | Conduct structured interviews | Interview Panel | Every interviewer completes an anchored scorecard with evidence notes; the same core questions were used. |
| 9 | Debrief and make the selection decision | Hiring Manager | The decision and its rationale are documented and stored; the selection-rate is checked against the 4/5 rule. |
| 10 | Complete reference checks | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | References are completed with consistent questions and results are recorded. |
| 11 | Approve compensation and extend a conditional verbal offer | Recruiter / Talent Acquisition | Compensation is approved and within band; the conditional verbal offer and its terms are confirmed by email. |
| 12 | Candidate response and negotiation | Candidate | The candidate's response is recorded; any negotiated changes are captured before proceeding. |
| 13 | Disclose, authorize, and run the background check | HR Business Partner | A signed standalone disclosure and authorization are on file before the report is ordered; the report is stored securely. |
| 14 | Adjudicate the results | HR Business Partner | The adjudication applies job-relatedness and an individualized assessment; the rationale is documented. |
| 15 | Run FCRA adverse action (only if withdrawing) | HR Business Partner | Both notices are documented with dates and the waiting period is observed; no irreversible internal action is taken during the waiting period. |
| 16 | Extend the formal written offer | HR Business Partner | The sent offer matches approved terms; non-standard terms were reviewed by Legal; an acceptance deadline is set. |
| 17 | Countersign and execute the agreement | HR Business Partner | Candidate signature and employer countersignature are on file; the start date is confirmed. |
| 18 | Initiate right-to-work and hand off to onboarding | HR Business Partner | Form I-9 is initiated and the onboarding handoff package is delivered to the onboarding owner. |

**Escalation:** If budget is contested, escalate to the department head and HRBP.
