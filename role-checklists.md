# Role checklists — Hire for an open role: requisition to signed contract

_SOP-HR-002 · v1.0.0_

## Hiring Manager
_Owns the role, its requirements, and the final selection decision; Defines job-related criteria and participates in structured interviews_

- [ ] **S1** Open and document the requisition
  - [ ] verified: A requisition record exists in the ATS with role, level, reporting line, and business case.
- [ ] **S9** Debrief and make the selection decision
  - [ ] verified: The decision and its rationale are documented and stored; the selection-rate is checked against the 4/5 rule.

## Recruiter / Talent Acquisition
_Drives the process end to end and owns the candidate experience; Sources, screens, coordinates interviews, and extends offers_

- [ ] **S3** Finalize the job description and selection criteria
  - [ ] verified: Every listed requirement is job-related and consistent with business necessity; the scorecard defines competencies with behavioral anchors.
- [ ] **S4** Source candidates
  - [ ] verified: The role is posted to all required channels and a candidate pipeline is building in the ATS.
- [ ] **S5** Screen applications
  - [ ] verified: All applicants are screened against the same criteria and screening decisions are logged.
- [ ] **S6** Recruiter phone screen
  - [ ] verified: Each candidate is screened with the same core questions; notes are recorded in the ATS.
- [ ] **S7** Administer job-related assessments
  - [ ] verified: The same assessment is sent to all candidates for the role and results are recorded.
- [ ] **S10** Complete reference checks
  - [ ] verified: References are completed with consistent questions and results are recorded.
- [ ] **S11** Approve compensation and extend a conditional verbal offer
  - [ ] verified: Compensation is approved and within band; the conditional verbal offer and its terms are confirmed by email.

## HR Business Partner
_Oversees process compliance, recordkeeping, and offer/contract governance; Owns FCRA adverse action and right-to-work verification_

- [ ] **S13** Disclose, authorize, and run the background check
  - [ ] verified: A signed standalone disclosure and authorization are on file before the report is ordered; the report is stored securely.
- [ ] **S14** Adjudicate the results
  - [ ] verified: The adjudication applies job-relatedness and an individualized assessment; the rationale is documented.
- [ ] **S15** Run FCRA adverse action (only if withdrawing)
  - [ ] verified: Both notices are documented with dates and the waiting period is observed; no irreversible internal action is taken during the waiting period.
- [ ] **S16** Extend the formal written offer
  - [ ] verified: The sent offer matches approved terms; non-standard terms were reviewed by Legal; an acceptance deadline is set.
- [ ] **S17** Countersign and execute the agreement
  - [ ] verified: Candidate signature and employer countersignature are on file; the start date is confirmed.
- [ ] **S18** Initiate right-to-work and hand off to onboarding
  - [ ] verified: Form I-9 is initiated and the onboarding handoff package is delivered to the onboarding owner.

## Interview Panel
_Conducts structured interviews and completes anchored scorecards; Provides evidence-based, job-related evaluations_

- [ ] **S8** Conduct structured interviews
  - [ ] verified: Every interviewer completes an anchored scorecard with evidence notes; the same core questions were used.

## Compensation / Finance Approver
_Approves headcount, budget, and the compensation package; Confirms the offer sits within the approved band_

- [ ] **S2** Approve headcount and budget
  - [ ] verified: The requisition shows an approval record with approver, date, and budgeted band.

## Candidate
_External party; responds to the offer and completes required documents_

- [ ] **S12** Candidate response and negotiation
  - [ ] verified: The candidate's response is recorded; any negotiated changes are captured before proceeding.
